<h2 id="vote">Vote prediction example</h2>
<p>Suppose I am standing for election in a large population, and that there are two candidates. I run a poll over 100 people, and 51 say they will vote for me and 49 say they won’t. What is the chance that I will win the election?</p>
Here are the poll results:
